# AI Readiness Resources

Public resources, notes and practical frameworks on AI readiness, data strategy and business intelligence for SMEs and professionals.

## About Future Data

Future Data Software Online Ltd is a UK-registered technology and consulting company focused on AI readiness, data strategy, business intelligence, KPI architecture and digital transformation.

The company supports professionals, small businesses and organisations in understanding how data, automation and AI-enabled processes can improve decision-making, operational planning, market analysis and long-term business performance.

## Repository Purpose

This repository will gradually host public-facing resources related to:

- AI readiness assessment
- Data strategy for SMEs
- Business intelligence and KPI architecture
- Digital transformation planning
- Market intelligence and decision-support methods
- Responsible and practical use of AI in business processes

## Current Status

This repository is in its initial setup phase. Materials will be added progressively as part of Future Data’s public knowledge infrastructure.
